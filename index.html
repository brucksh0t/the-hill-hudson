The estate, in order of the night

From the barn to the fire

01 · Ceremony & reception

The Palladian Barn, 1801

Once the estate’s carriage barn, now a two-level venue with stalls on the first floor and a vast event space on the second. If it rains, the ceremony moves downstairs or upstairs.

02 · Dinner

75 to 300 guests

The Hill lists the barn for 75 to 300 guests for ceremony and reception. String lights in the barn come with it.

03 · Cocktails or the after-party

The Art Barn

The old blacksmith’s shop, where the estate’s carriages were repaired, with lovely windows and a long farm table.

Fire
pit

04 · Late

By the fire pit

Wind the evening down by the fire. The Hill says to ask about setting one up. Call to confirm: 845-758-9002.

As listed by The Hill

What’s included

  • 300 padded white chairs, 25 round tables, 12 rectangular tables and three farm tables
  • String lights in the Palladian Barn
  • Indoor washrooms for up to 100 people, including a handicap-accessible bathroom
  • Lights, fans, indoor and outdoor furniture, signs, trellises, chuppahs and games
  • A bridal day cottage, plus a private groom’s day room in the barn
  • Access two days before and the day after, for setup and cleanup
  • A site manager and two staff members on the day
  • A kid-friendly area with a miniature train, and pets welcome

Pricing

The Hill quotes custom pricing for each wedding and doesn’t publish rates. This concept doesn’t estimate one.

Good to know

Season
May through October.
Weddings per year
One per weekend, and no more than 22 a year.
Capacity
Up to 300 in the barn for ceremony and reception.
Music
Amplified until 10:30 pm, un-amplified until 11:00 pm.
Vendors
The Hill shares a vendor list, or you can bring your own.
Getting here
About two hours from New York City by car, or take Amtrak to Hudson. Minutes from Hudson’s hotels and B&Bs.
History
Created in 1801 by Henry Walter Livingston. Lafayette was among its guests.
Officiant
Reverend Betsy, an ordained Interfaith Minister, is available to officiate.

All from The Hill’s own website. Policies may change for 2027. Call to confirm: 845-758-9002.
